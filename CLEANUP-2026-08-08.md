## Facts from the deleted docs that are not in `CLAUDE.md`

- **The standing goal, in the user's words:** a run "produces good trajectories for the groups (as
  lambda increases, they are progressively driven to zero. not all at the same time, one by one.
  accuracy or model compatibility degrades smoothly as lambda increases)", and is "significantly
  faster than the baseline using scipy.minimize and syncing to host (approx 24h now)".
- **The target shape was seen once.** `results_v7` `markers/both`, 38 λ: 13/13 flat to λ=459, then
  12→11→…→1→0 one at a time, R² 0.9998 → 0.859 at 4 groups, 3 unconverged λ all inside the flat
  region. That path is deleted and was produced under optimistix, so it is a memory of a shape, not
  a baseline.
- **Two open defects were diagnosed and never fixed**, both still live in `run.py`:
  1. *Refinement fits rubber-stamp their warmstart.* In the v7 `forces/both` tail, four consecutive
     λ converged in **3** ADMM iterations — the earliest the ramp permits — at 8 active, then one λ
     that ran 158 iterations landed at 4 active with a *higher* R². Fits were passing the residual
     test before the solution had moved. Fix direction: require `z` to have actually moved before
     the convergence break can fire. (Multi-start makes this detectable, not absent.)
  2. *Adjacent λ in the final path come from different continuation chains.* Refinement warmstarts
     each bisection from `states[lo]`, the denser end of its own interval; the result is then
     printed beside a λ reached down a different chain, which is what produced non-monotone active
     counts (λ=3014 at 1 active, λ=3171 at 2). Fix direction: process refinement points in
     increasing λ, warmstart each from the nearest already-fitted lower λ.
- **The near-zero-λ dual band** (cost, not correctness): seven consecutive λ in 0.2166…2.467 burned
  the full 400 iterations at full support, then λ=3.70 converged in 8, with a *larger* primal
  residual. The binding test is the dual one, and just above λ=0 `‖u‖` is small enough that
  `tol·rho·‖u‖` collapses toward the absolute floor. Do not fix by loosening `tol` globally; the
  dual floor should be tied to the inner solver's accuracy. R² was flat across the band, so it cost
  2800 iterations and no path damage.
- **The provenance failure, in one line:** seven result generations all recorded
  `git_revision = b7c4dc6` because nothing was committed between them, so nothing distinguishes them
  in the ledger. `run.py` now records `git_dirty` and warns; the discipline is to commit before
  submitting.
- **Speed history** (optimistix era, one knot, A100, not comparable across rows — the honest
  comparison is the last two): scipy baseline 30.2 s/ADMM-iteration; optimistix 35 iters / 488.7 s;
  L-BFGS-B `maxiter=5 ls=5` 16 iters / 67.4 s at a better log-likelihood.
- **Never approved, do not start:** the P1 methodology track (train/test split change, λ selection,
  debiasing, NLPD). It is listed here only so it is not rediscovered as new. The even/odd split
  means test is not independent of train; that is known debt.
- **Multi-GPU output sharding** was noted as the obvious next parallelism lever (`shard_map` over
  outputs, estimated ~2× at 2 GPUs) and never measured. `CLAUDE.md` prefers batching over *starts*.

## History and motivation, moved out of `CLAUDE.md`

`CLAUDE.md` now states rules without their backstory. The backstory is here. Everything in this
section is *why* a rule exists, not what the code does today.

### Why no logged number is evidence

- **Every result pickle ever written was deleted on 2026-08-04** — seven generations, 416 MB, plus
  74 MB deleted mid-flight while an array was still writing it. Nothing measured before that date
  can be recomputed without rerunning.
- **The benchmark tables were never mutually comparable.** Cold-state repeats, warmstarted
  iterations, and compile-included fits were quoted against one another. The single defensible
  solver comparison (one knot, one warmstart, end to end) is L-BFGS-B `maxiter=5 ls=5` at 67.4 s /
  16 ADMM iterations against optimistix at 488.7 s / 35 — and even that is one knot, not a path.
- **Several tuned defaults rest on obsolete measurements** — `history_length=40`, chunk sizing,
  inner tolerances — taken against older versions of the problem: different box, different solver,
  different parametrization. Plausible starting points to re-measure, not validated settings.
- The **mathematical** claims (evenness, absorbing death, box dynamics) are derivations checkable
  from the code, so they survived the deletion. The **numbers** did not.

### Why the current design was chosen (2026-08-07)

The restructure picked ADMM + multi-start + post-hoc certificates over the two alternatives, a
direct penalized solve and greedy backward elimination — both deferred, not rejected on evidence.

- **Multi-start exists because continuation bias is structural, not incidental.** Death is
  absorbing, so a chained warmstart can only lose groups; the dense λ=0 start is the only thing that
  can win one back. `tests/test_multistart.py` proves a resurrection.
- **`kkt_certificate` exists because `converged=True` was caught grazing its own tolerance.**
  Recomputed off the pickles before deletion: late-path primal residuals sat at ratios 0.9947–0.9996
  of tolerance rather than below it, so the tolerance — not the optimum — decided where a fit
  stopped and which group died next. The dual test reduces to `‖Δz‖ < tol·‖u‖` (rho cancels), so it
  *loosens* as `‖u‖` grows along the path. And rho tracks λ through the adaptation rule, so the
  ratio `λ/rho` that actually kills groups is non-monotone even when λ is monotone — part of the λ
  axis was illusory.
- **Dead-group certification is vacuous** (evenness makes the subgradient slack trivially λ);
  multi-start is the compensation for that, not an extra.
- **The λ-grid walk and rho adaptation are still restructure material.** Multi-start makes their
  biases *detected* rather than assumed away; the rho/λ entanglement stands.
- **Optimistix was removed on one knot's evidence plus a user decision.** The planned path-level A/B
  never ran, so certificate quality over a full lbfgsb path was unmeasured at the time of removal.
  If certificates fail systematically on the first real generation, suspect the inner budget before
  the algorithm.

### Things that were tried and failed — the reason behind each prohibition

- **`rho_floor`** held every group a fixed factor away from its own death threshold and froze whole
  paths at full support. It conflated a global pathology (all x-update targets negative at once)
  with the legitimate death of one marginal group.
- **A clipped nugget.** `w` was once a clipped column of `x`; any output noisier than the ceiling
  then held the primal residual above tol at *every* λ, so no fit could converge.
- **Over-relaxation** (`x_hat = α·x + (1-α)·z`, removed 2026-08-07) leaves the box whenever `x` and
  `z` straddle, feeding `u` the overshoot and reopening the mirror well. At λ=0, where the prox is
  the identity, it turns the primal residual into a noise meter for the x-update's jitter
  (`r = (α-1)‖x - z_prev‖`) with a floor no budget crosses. `linear.py` ran `α=1.6` legitimately —
  no box, convex — and lost it anyway, a pure speed loss accepted to keep `glasso_admm.py`
  single-path.
- **Gating rho adaptation on residuals clearing `eps_abs`** was tried instead of the λ=0 handoff
  reset, and burns the whole budget at λ=0.
- **Unclamped rho adaptation** let an irreducible primal floor feed the ×2 rule up to rho ~1e22,
  where the augmented term swamps float64.
- **The downward λ walk.** Chaining a fit onto a sparser neighbour produced whole paths at 0 active,
  because death is absorbing.
- **`%013.6f` λ filenames** collapsed every λ below ~5e-7 onto the λ=0 pickle. The cache key is the
  parsed λ value, so any rounding format silently merges knots.
- **Seeding a fresh generation from an old one carries pathology across it** — v7 seeded from v6's
  frozen pickles reproduced the freeze exactly. A code fix needs a fresh grid, not a resumed one.
- **Legacy pickle converters** were dropped 2026-08-07 and no old-format pickle exists. Reading one
  format's `w` as another's is a wrong-nugget bug that leaves no trace, which is why a
  format mismatch cold-starts instead of warmstarting.

### Motivation notes worth keeping

- **Why the box on `theta` at all:** it is load-bearing dynamically and free statistically.
  Unconstrained, a dead group's fixed point requires `u = 0` exactly (measure zero), and ADMM
  limit-cycles between the two mirror wells instead.
- **Why `relu` sits in the likelihood and not the augmented term:** at a fixed point the x-update
  target is `z_g (1 - l1/(rho‖z_g‖))`, which goes negative as a group approaches death, and on an
  even objective that makes the reflected well the deeper one. Relu makes the negative orthant a
  plateau (C1 across the boundary, since evenness forces zero gradient there) while the raw `theta`
  in the augmented term still pulls a stray negative coordinate back. If relu saw the augmented term
  too, the orthant would be entirely flat and nothing could escape it.
- **Why `eps_abs = sqrt(p)·EPS`** (Boyd §3.3.1): both residuals are norms of p-element vectors, and
  at λ=0 the dual criterion has no relative term left, so an unscaled floor asks the inexact
  x-update for a residual below its own noise.
- **Why rho is reset at the λ=0 handoff rather than gated:** at λ=0 the prox is the identity, `u ≡ 0`,
  the primal residual is identically zero, and `dual > 10·primal` fires every iteration — rho
  decaying toward `RHO_MIN` is *locally correct* there, and the only way λ=0's dual test passes at
  all. The damage is only at the handoff, where a warmstart at rho≈1e-6 has a prox threshold that
  kills every group on contact.
- **Why `lax.scan` and not vmap for prediction:** one n×n kernel live at a time, deliberate OOM
  avoidance.
- **Why float64 is non-negotiable:** the kernel computes squared distance as
  `|z1|² + |z2|² - 2 z1·z2` — one matmul, but it cancels.
- **Why the column interleaving is load-bearing:** markers are built `[LCAL1, RCAL1, LCUB, RCUB, …]`,
  left/right of the same marker adjacent, which is the only reason `group_size=6` means one marker
  across both feet. Reordering columns silently redefines a group.
- **Why batch scripts are committed** despite `*.slurm` being gitignored (`!slurm/*.slurm`
  overrides): they are the only record of what a generation ran with — see the provenance failure
  above.
